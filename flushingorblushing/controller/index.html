A LITTLE TABLE TALK

Three moves. Use them wisely.

See
Look at one player’s secret role card. Don’t reveal it to anyone else.
Add
Draw two cards into the hand you’re holding.
Take
Discard one card from the center, then draw one card into your held hand.

Each card can be used only once per game. An action replaces playing a card on your turn.

Tap a card when you take that action at the table. Perform the action with your physical deck.

Start a new game?
