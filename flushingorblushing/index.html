THE HOUSE RULES

A shared hand.
A secret ambition.

A standard 52-card deck. No jokers.
The guide below uses the four-player setup.

01

Keep your suit close.

Deal everyone a secret role card. Announce its rank, never its suit. The highest rank starts. Deal that player and the player two seats clockwise seven cards each.

02

Play. Act. Pass.

The starting player must play a card first. After that, play one card into the center or use an action instead. After both hand-holders take a turn, pass both hands clockwise together.

03

Let the cards decide.

A straight ends the round. Otherwise, finish when play returns to the starter with seven cards in the center. If your role wins, collect a winning chip.

At seven cards, use an action or skip if none remain. Otherwise, you cannot willingly skip. Each action takes your whole turn.

YOUR SUIT SETS YOUR GOAL

Everyone has an angle.

♠

Spades

♣

Clubs

Four or more face cards

♦

Diamonds

Flush or three of a kind

♥

Hearts

Straight, full house or better

A straight flush goes to Hearts, not Diamonds. If a straight ends the round but a flush is the best hand, score the flush.
