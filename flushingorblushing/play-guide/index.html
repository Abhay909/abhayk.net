POKER HANDS · STRONGEST TO WEAKEST

Know what you’re building.

  1. Royal flush

    Ace, King, Queen, Jack, and Ten of the exact same suit.

  2. Straight flush

    Five cards in a row, all in the same suit.

  3. Four of a kind (quads)

    Four cards of the same rank plus one extra card.

  4. Full house

    Three cards of one rank and a pair of another rank.

  5. Flush

    Five cards of the same suit, not in a row.

  6. Straight

    Five cards in a row, with mixed suits.

  7. Three of a kind (trips / set)

    Three cards of the same rank plus two side cards.

  8. Two pair

    Two separate pairs plus a fifth card.

  9. One pair

    One pair plus three other unpaired cards.

  10. High card

    No matching hand; the highest single card plays.
