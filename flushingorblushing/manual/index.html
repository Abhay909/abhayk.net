FLUSHING OR BLUSHING

The written manual

3–6 players · 10–20 minutes
One shared hand. A secret goal for every player.

01 · FOUR-PLAYER SETUP

Keep your role secret.

  1. Deal each player one card from the top of a standard 52-card deck, with jokers removed. This is their role card. Keep it secret.
  2. Everyone announces the number or rank of their role card, never its suit.
  3. Deal the player with the highest rank a hand of seven cards. That player is Player 1 and goes first.
  4. Count two players clockwise from Player 1. Deal that player seven cards too.

Give each player one See, Add, and Take action card, or open the online action cards on their phone. Use chips to track actions and winning chips to record wins.

Playing with three, five, or six people? Use the variant setup below.

02 · HOW TO PLAY

Build the hand together.

Your secret role’s suit sets your win condition. Everyone drafts a common hand in the center, trying to steer it toward their own goal.

  1. Player 1 chooses a card from their hand and lays it in the center. Player 1 must play a card on their first turn.
  2. After that first turn, a hand-holder either plays one card into the center or uses an available action. An action takes the whole turn.
  3. The other player holding a hand then plays a card or takes an action.
  4. After both players have acted, pass both hands clockwise together. Repeat this turn cycle.
When does the round end?

A straight ends the round. Otherwise, when play returns to Player 1, check the center: if seven cards remain, the round ends. If fewer than seven remain, keep playing until Player 1’s turn returns with seven cards down.

Once seven cards are in the center, do not add another central card. Players can still take actions before the round ends, including Take to remove a card.

If seven cards are down and you have no actions left, skip your turn. Otherwise, you cannot willingly skip, except in the six-player variant.

03 · ACTIONS

One use each, per round.

Use each action once per round and mark it with a chip or the online controller. When you take an action, do not also lay down a card.

See
Look at one player’s role card. You may not say anything about it or reveal it to anyone else.
Add
Draw two cards into the hand you are holding.
Take
Remove and discard one card from the central hand. Then draw one card into the hand you are holding.

The controller tracks your actions while you play with physical cards. At the start of a fresh round, confirm New game to restore your three action cards.

04 · ROLES & WINNING

Your suit sets your goal.

♠ Spades
One pair.
♣ Clubs
Four or more face cards.
♦ Diamonds
A flush or three of a kind.
♥ Hearts
A straight, full house, or better.

When the round ends, if your role’s win condition is satisfied, take a winning chip.

A straight ends the round. A straight flush is a win for Hearts, not Diamonds. See the play guide for poker hand definitions.

05 · VARIANT PLAY

A seat for everyone.

Three players

Deal one 14-card hand to Player 1. Pass that hand clockwise. Keep the other rules the same.

Five players

Use the standard setup, but change the order of play and passing:

  1. Player 1 acts and passes their hand to the person on their left, who becomes Player 3.
  2. Player 2 acts and passes their hand to the person on their left, who becomes Player 4.
  3. Player 3 acts and passes their hand to Player 2.
  4. Player 4 acts and passes their hand to Player 5.
  5. Player 5 acts and passes their hand to Player 1, who acts next.

Six players

Deal five cards into each hand instead of seven. Add a third hand, dealt to the player two seats to the left of Player 2. Continue the normal turn cycle.

With six players, anyone may choose to pass and skip their turn at any time.

06 · Q&A

A couple of table rulings.

A straight ends the round, but the best hand is a flush. What happens?

The hand becomes a flush, but the straight still ends the round. Score the flush.

Do Hearts and Diamonds both win on a straight flush?

No. Hearts win; Diamonds do not.

Have fun. Flush don’t blush!
